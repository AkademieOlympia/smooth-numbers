## The Two Possible Worlds (Falsifiability)

The gap-pair test creates genuine falsifiability with two outcomes:

### World 1: Projection Invariance
```
A₁(Δr) ≈ A₂(Δr) ≈ A₃(Δr)
```

**Interpretation**: New object isolated  
**Scientific value**: High (proceed to Phase 4)  
**Publication**: Position paper + results paper  

### World 2: Projection Dependence
```
A₁(Δr) ≠ A₂(Δr) ≠ A₃(Δr)
```

**Interpretation**: Deeper structure needed  
**Scientific value**: High (deeper invariant must be sought)  
**Publication**: Methodological paper on projection sensitivity  

**Both outcomes are scientifically valuable.**

This is **genuine falsifiability**:
- Not "success vs. failure"
- But "direct path vs. additional abstraction layer"
