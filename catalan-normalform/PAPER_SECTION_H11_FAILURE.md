### 5.3 A Failed Octonionic Embedding and the Remaining Tamari-Associator Core

During the development of this research program, we investigated a potential connection between EABC classes and octonionic algebras (H11, Hurwitz-Catalan hierarchy). This investigation **failed**, but the failure is instructive.

#### The Original Hypothesis

The idea was to map EABC classes to octonions:

E → 1, A → i, B → j, C → k

and use the non-associativity of octonions to assign meaning to Catalan tree structures via the associator:

[a,b,c] = (ab)c - a(bc)

#### The Structural Flaw

The proposed mapping remains entirely within the **quaternionic subalgebra** ℍ ⊂ 𝕆. Since quaternions are associative, we have [a,b,c] = 0 for all triples. The entire non-associativity vanishes.

This is not a technical detail but a **structural impossibility**: the embedding cannot produce what it was designed to measure.

#### What Remains

The failure revealed a **robust mathematical core** independent of EABC:

**The Catalan–Tamari–Associator Connection:**
- Catalan numbers count complete parenthesizations
- The Tamari lattice describes rotations between parenthesizations
- In non-associative algebras, the associator [a,b,c] measures exactly the deviation changed by a Tamari rotation

This connection is **established mathematics** and could be developed independently as a geometric study of non-associative structures.

#### Lesson Learned

Not every geometric analogy survives the transition to algebra. The value of documenting this failure lies in demonstrating that the research program:

1. Tests its own hypotheses
2. Documents negative results explicitly
3. Distinguishes robust mathematical results from speculative extensions
4. Updates priorities based on evidence

#### Future Direction

If octonionic structures become relevant again, they require:
1. A corrected embedding that genuinely escapes all quaternionic subalgebras
2. Proof that EABC classes have a natural relationship to the Fano plane of octonions
3. Empirical evidence that such structures carry arithmetic information

Without these prerequisites, H11 remains speculative (Level D in our evidence hierarchy).
